## Design Context

### Users

MySearch Proxy Console serves operators who run shared search infrastructure for themselves, a team, or a community deployment. Their primary jobs are checking provider health, switching workspaces, managing tokens and API keys, monitoring quota and usage, copying integration settings, and diagnosing failures quickly.

### Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and technical. The interface should feel like a precise operations desk rather than a marketing landing page or a generic enterprise admin template.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a warm editorial operations-console direction: tinted paper-like neutrals, restrained teal as the brand accent, provider-specific status colors, compact information density, and deliberate asymmetry. Support both light and dark themes. Avoid decorative glassmorphism, repeated card grids, oversized marketing copy, neon-on-dark styling, and redundant explanatory text.

### Design Principles

- Put live operational status and the current workspace above promotional explanation.
- Reveal one provider workspace at a time and use progressive disclosure for detailed maintenance tools.
- Use typography, whitespace, rules, and alignment before adding another card or shadow.
- Keep provider identity and status distinguishable by label and icon as well as color.
- Preserve full keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, and mobile task parity.
